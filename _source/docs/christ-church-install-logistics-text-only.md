## What Is Known From Chris's Rough Draft

Scaffolding will be built around the perimeter of the sanctuary approximately 10 feet out from the roof eaves. The scaffold should include a platform, three-rail guardrail system, and toe board.

Additional scaffolding will be erected at the north end of the sanctuary. This north-end scaffold is intended to create a multi-platform condition that follows the angle of the sanctuary roof line up toward the bell station.

Scaffolding will also be erected on all sides of the steeple and extend to the top. Platforms and a three-tier railing system will be used for PPE tie-offs and material landing spots.

Multiple garbage chutes will be attached to the steeple scaffolding. Those chutes will extend down to a dumpster placed adjacent to the base of the scaffolding.

## Phase 1: Steeple Tear-Off And Rebuild

Expected timing: Monday, August 3 through Friday, August 7, 2026

Each side of the steeple is approximately 5 squares.

Each pallet of Brava is approximately 5.8 squares.

Initial steeple material load: 4 pallets of Brava.

A crane will be placed next to the base scaffolding at the north end of the sanctuary. Its purpose is to lift material from the ground to the scaffolding surrounding the steeple.

Crane operator hours are expected to be 7:00 AM to 3:30 PM.

The crew is expected to complete 1 to 2 sections of the steeple per day.

After the steeple is complete, the steeple scaffolding comes down and the crane leaves.

## Phase 2: Sanctuary Tear-Off And Rebuild

Expected timing: begins after Phase 1, currently assumed to start Monday, August 10, 2026

The sanctuary will be worked in sections, one section at a time.

Plywood will be placed over stained-glass windows when nearby work creates exposure risk. Plywood should be removed before any sanctuary services or Sunday services.

Material will be loaded onto adjacent flat roofs as needed for each active sanctuary section.

A forklift will move material from the church-approved staging area in the marked parking lot to the base scaffold or flat roof loading point.
