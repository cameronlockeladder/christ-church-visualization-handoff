      Material Staging

      Material staging starts in the dedicated parking lot and is then moved to the flat roof. A boom truck is brought out only when material needs to be lifted.

      Stained Glass Protection

      Plywood protects the stained glass during work. Boards are removed during service times — they are not permanently fixed to the wall. The stained glass remains functional for Sunday and any additional services.

      Phase 2 Inspection

      During the sanctuary phase, the roof deck is inspected section by section. Any deck-level mitigation is handled while that section is exposed, before new Brava cedar is installed.
