# Project Sequence Notes

These notes are the content source for the timeline. Keep the wording plain enough for a layperson.

## Dates And Scope

### 07/06/2026 - 07/31/2026

Scaffolding setup.

This is site preparation before active roof tear-off begins.

### 08/03/2026 - 08/07/2026

Steeple work.

- Tear off existing cedar on the steeple.
- Begin installation of new Brava cedar.
- Existing roof material is disposed through a chute to the dumpster.

### Upon Completion Of Steeple

Site transition.

- Steeple scaffolding is removed.
- The crane departs and is no longer on the property.
- The dumpster remains to support sanctuary roof removal.
- Sanctuary scaffolding remains in place.

Important correction: scaffolding remains around the sanctuary until the steeple and sanctuary work are complete.

### 08/10/2026 - 08/28/2026

Phase 2 sanctuary roof work.

- Remove existing sanctuary roof section by section.
- Install new Brava cedar.
- Include all metal drip edge.
- Inspect existing roof deck during Phase 2.
- Perform mitigation during Phase 2 if needed.

## Material Staging

- Material staging starts in the dedicated parking lot.
- Material moves from the dedicated parking lot to the flat roof.
- The boom truck is brought out only to lift material.

## Stained Glass Protection

- Plywood protects stained glass during work.
- Boards are removed during service times.
- Boards are not permanently fixed to the wall.
- Stained glass remains functional for Sunday services and any additional services.
